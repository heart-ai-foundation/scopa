# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

SCOPA contributors, maintainers, reviewers, and community members are expected
to create a respectful, evidence-focused environment. Constructive disagreement
is welcome; harassment, intimidation, discrimination, doxxing, credential
exposure, and bad-faith disruption are not.

Examples of welcome behavior include:

- explaining technical claims with reproducible evidence;
- separating critique of an idea from judgment of a person;
- protecting vulnerability reports, personal data, and private evidence;
- acknowledging uncertainty, conflicts of interest, and mistakes; and
- helping newcomers understand project boundaries and contribution practices.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- abusive, sexualized, discriminatory, or threatening language or conduct;
- sustained disruption, trolling, or personal attacks;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- pressuring someone to reveal credentials or confidential evidence; and
- retaliating against a good-faith report or review finding.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when a person officially
represents SCOPA or Heart AI Foundation in project-related communication.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not litigate sensitive conduct reports in a public issue. Request a private
follow-up through the
[Heart AI Foundation contact page](https://empathyethicist.ai/contact/) with the
subject “SCOPA conduct report.” Do not include unnecessary personal or
confidential details in the initial message.

Project stewards may remove content, limit participation, reject contributions,
or ban participation when needed to protect the community. Reports will be
handled as privately as practical, with attention to conflicts of interest and
the safety of affected participants.
