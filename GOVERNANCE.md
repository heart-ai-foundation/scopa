# SCOPA governance

## Stewardship

The Heart AI Foundation is the project steward and release authority. The
Foundation maintains the canonical source, reviews contributions, protects the
free verification boundary, and decides release readiness.

## Decision model

Routine changes proceed through review, test evidence, and the active
Next-Prompt Protocol slice. Changes to scope locks, release licensing,
contribution terms, package naming, repository visibility, registry
publication, production deployment, or public claims require a recorded
decision and separate principal approval.

Maintainers may merge compatible contributions under Apache-2.0 after DCO
sign-off. There is no CLA in the approved developer-preview posture.

## Technical invariants

- Next-Prompt Protocol and the SCOPA runtime remain distinct.
- Scope, completion, and continuity do not replace the eight HEART control
  surfaces.
- Verification, replay verification, conformance fixtures, and supported
  adapters remain independently useful without a paid service.
- Adapter assurance is evidence-derived; uncontrolled routes force `partial`.
- Known-failing fixtures and non-claims are release artifacts, not internal
  inconveniences to remove.

## Consultation boundary

Paid work may cover profile design, governance installation, evidence-quality
review, regulated integration, and reconstructability assessment.
Consultation must not make SCOPA verification depend on a paid service or
restrict access to the truth of a verification result.

## Conduct and disputes

Participants should keep review evidence-focused, protect vulnerability and
personal data, and document conflicts of interest. The Foundation resolves
technical and release disputes, recording material governance decisions in the
project evidence trail.
